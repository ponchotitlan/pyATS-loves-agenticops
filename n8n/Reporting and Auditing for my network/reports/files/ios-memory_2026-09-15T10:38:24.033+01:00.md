# Network Assessment Report

## Executive Summary

A memory utilization assessment was conducted on **2026-09-15** across all IOS/IOS XE/IOS XR devices present in the testbed inventory. A total of **4 devices** were queried (2 routers, 2 switches — all running IOS XE on the IOL platform). ## Risks & Considerations

No memory-related risks are present at this time. All devices are well below the 80% alert threshold with over 652 MB of free Processor Pool memory on routers and over 669 MB on switches. No corrective action is warranted based on current data.

## Recommendations

No recommendations required. Memory utilization is healthy across all polled devices. As a standard operational practice:
- **Continue periodic monitoring** of the Processor Pool at least every 15–30 minutes via SNMP (`ciscoMemoryPoolUsed` OID) or `show processes memory` polling to detect gradual growth trends over time.
- **Establish a baseline** from today's values (~16.6% for routers, ~13.9% for switches) to detect future anomalies.
- If the environment scales (additional routing protocols, VRFs, crypto sessions), re-evaluate the 80% threshold and consider lowering it to **70%** as an early-warning trigger.
